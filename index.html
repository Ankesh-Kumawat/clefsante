The Three Energies

Understanding the Tridoshas

According to Ayurveda, every individual is unique and governed by three biological energies, or Doshas. True health lies in balancing these forces.

Vata Dosha

Air & Ether

Governs movement, respiration, blood flow, and nervous system signals. When balanced, it inspires creativity and flexibility. Out of balance, it causes anxiety and fatigue.

  • Characteristics: Thin, light, active, cold-sensitive
  • Imbalance sign: Dry skin, bloating, insomnia

Pitta Dosha

Fire & Water

Controls metabolism, digestion, body temperature, and critical processing. Balanced Pitta brings strong understanding, intelligence, and drive. Imbalances lead to anger and inflammation.

  • Characteristics: Medium build, warm, sharp mind
  • Imbalance sign: Heartburn, skin rashes, irritability

Kapha Dosha

Earth & Water

Maintains body structure, lubrication, joints, and fluid balance. A balanced Kapha exhibits love, patience, stability, and stamina. Excess Kapha results in lethargy and weight gain.

  • Characteristics: Solid build, calm, deliberate
  • Imbalance sign: Congestion, sluggishness, depression
