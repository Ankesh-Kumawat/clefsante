Abhyanga: Self-Oil Massage

Sanskrit word 'Sneha' means both 'oil' and 'love'. Saturating your body with warm oil calms the nervous system and slows skin aging.

Advertisement

Abhyanga is the foundational daily ritual of warm herbal oil application. In ancient texts, regular oil massage is praised for increasing longevity, softening skin, invigorating nerve endings, and promoting deep restful sleep by grounding Vata dosha.

How to Perform Abhyanga at Home

1. Warm the Oil: Place your oil bottle in a cup of hot water for 3-5 minutes until pleasantly warm.

2. Long Strokes on Limbs, Circular on Joints: Use long sweeping strokes along arms and legs toward the heart. Use gentle clockwise circular motions on joints (shoulders, knees, ankles) and abdomen.

3. Rest for 15 Minutes: Allow the oil to penetrate skin layers into underlying muscular tissue before taking a warm shower or bath.
