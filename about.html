Ayurveda: The Science of Longevity

Ayurveda is derived from two Sanskrit words: Ayur meaning life, and Veda meaning science or knowledge. Rather than treating symptoms superficially, it treats the individual as an integrated system of mind, body, environment, and sensory awareness.

At Clefsanté, we believe that true health is not merely the absence of disease. It is the dynamic expression of biological balance, clarity of cognitive functions, and sensory harmony.

"Swasthyasya Swasthya Rakshanam, Aturasya Vikara Prashamanam Cha."
— Charaka Samhita (To protect the health of the healthy, and pacify disease in the sick).

Core Teachings

  • 🌿
    Prakriti (Your Constitution)

    The unique ratio of five universal elements (Space, Air, Fire, Water, Earth) fixed at the time of your conception.

  • 🔥
    Agni (Digestive Fire)

    The metabolic engine that governs digestion, cellular transformation, nutrient absorption, and body temperature.

  • 🍃
    Dinacharya (Daily Flow)

    Aligning waking, hygiene, eating, and sleep windows with the solar clock to maximize natural hormone balance.
