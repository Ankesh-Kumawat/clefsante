Why Flow With Nature?

According to Ayurvedic text Ashtanga Hridaya, every 24-hour cycle is split into six zones governed by Vata, Pitta, and Kapha energies. By adjusting our diet, sleep, and physical exertions to fit these slots, we prevent stress hormones (cortisol) from spikes.

For example, eating our largest meal at noon aligns with the solar peak (when Pitta is strongest in the environment). Waking before dawn lets us absorb the crisp Vata momentum, triggering natural morning bowel movements without needing stimulants like coffee.

Simple Habits to Start:

  • ✓ Warm Water Sip: Drink 1 glass of warm water on an empty stomach to sweep away colon toxins.
  • ✓ Oil Pulling: Swish 1 tbsp of sesame or coconut oil for 10 minutes to strengthen gums and jaw.
  • ✓ Early Sleep: Turn off digital screens by 9:30 PM to optimize liver detox hours.
